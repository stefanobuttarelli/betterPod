betterPod Share for Mac

Send songs and albums from your Mac to a betterPod with an 8‑digit code.

Download for MacmacOS 13 or later

For Apple silicon and Intel. The app isn't signed by Apple yet: the first time, open it, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (on macOS 13 and 14: right-click it → Open). After that it updates itself.
