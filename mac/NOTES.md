**betterPod Share for Mac** sends songs and albums from your Mac to a betterPod, over the internet or the same Wi-Fi, with an 8-digit code. It works with every betterPod from 0.69 on (Knulli and Miyoo).

1. Drop songs or album folders on the window (or on the app's icon), or click Choose….
2. Click Share: an 8-digit code shows.
3. On the betterPod: Music → Receive, and the eight digits.

A whole album, a few songs, or songs from several albums: they arrive as albums, each with its cover (several albums go with one code, in a folder of their own under Music/Shared). betterPod plays MP3, FLAC, Ogg, WAV, AIFF and M4A; a Miyoo plays them all but M4A, and says so before anything is sent.

**Opening it the first time.** The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it at first:

- macOS 15 and later: open it, click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → "betterPod Share was blocked" → Open Anyway.
- macOS 13 and 14: right-click the app → Open → Open.

For a Mac with Apple silicon or Intel, macOS 13 or later.
